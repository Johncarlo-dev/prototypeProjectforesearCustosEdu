ABOUT THE PROJECT

About CustosEdu

CustosEdu is a digital academic document management system designed to help students organize and access educational materials.

Meaning of CustosEdu

The name comes from the Latin word "Custos," meaning guardian or keeper, and "Edu," referring to education.

Purpose

The system provides a simple way to store, search, view, and download academic files through a digital vault.

CustosEdu is a school project prototype. Accounts and files are stored locally in the browser.
